# 📚 Educational & Classroom Guide

**My World** is designed in alignment with modern STEM and Social Studies pedagogical principles to support teachers, homeschoolers, and parents.

---

## 🎯 Core Learning Objectives

1. **Spatial Cognitive Mapping**: Rotating a 3D spherical globe enables children to grasp genuine continental shapes and spatial relationships, overcoming the distortions inherent in flat 2D Mercator maps.
2. **Deductive Geographical Reasoning**: By grouping multiple-choice options regionally, learners evaluate geographical proximity rather than memorizing isolated text strings.
3. **Timezone & Planetary Mechanics**: Live local time clocks help children intuitively grasp Earth's rotation relative to the Sun.
4. **Maritime Literacy**: Identifying closed seas and oceans fosters environmental awareness and geographical literacy beyond land borders.
5. **Multilingual Exploration**: Comparing country names across scripts (Latin, Katakana, Hanzi, Arabic) introduces global linguistics in a fun, pressure-free context.

---

## 🏫 Suggested Classroom Activities

### Activity 1: The Fast-Paced Capitals Relay (Grades 3-6)
- Students take turns answering questions in **Medium Mode** (10s limit).
- Teams earn bonus multipliers by spotting and zapping UFOs.
- High scores are saved to the Top 10 Leaderboard for friendly classroom competition.

### Activity 2: World Explorer Passport (Grades 1-4)
- Using **Exploration Mode**, students pick 5 countries across 5 different continents.
- Students record each nation's capital, population, currency symbol, and check the live local time clock to calculate time differences with their hometown.
